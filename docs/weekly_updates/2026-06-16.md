## The fellowship arc

The project began in April 2026 with a one-line ask — *make state lobbying disclosure data usable for democracy measurement* — and ends with three artifacts on `main`, an extraction pipeline that reads statutes faithfully, a backend prototype that serves real data, and 22 archived research lines documenting what was tried and why. Where it ended is meaningfully different from where it began, and worth saying out loud before listing what shipped.

### What changed about the question

The April framing assumed the path forward was *a rubric*: pick or build one, score 50 states, ship a ranking. The literature ruled that out by mid-May. **Newmark 2017's r = 0.04 correlation between CPI's and PRI's disclosure scores** — two of the most-cited rubrics in the field, purporting to measure the same thing, essentially unrelated — is not a measurement disagreement to arbitrate. It is evidence that *no single rubric should be privileged* as the project's foundation. The project pivoted accordingly: build a rubric-agnostic data layer (the **compendium** + per-state **StateMasterRecords**), and let downstream consumers — journalists, researchers, activists — apply their own weights. **No "Corda Rubric" is published or claimed.** Compendium 2.0 (the typed-cell schema) is built from nine source rubrics on even footing; each rubric's scoring rule lives as a deterministic projection helper that translates the cells into that rubric's published vocabulary as a sanity check.

### What shipped (in dependency order)

1. **Compendium 2.0 — the rubric-agnostic data contract.** Merged to `main` 2026-05-14 (`cac1469`); promoted to repo-level `compendium/` later that day. **181 typed-cell rows**, frozen from `compendium-source-extracts`, built across nine source frameworks (PRI 2010, CPI 2015, CPI Hired Guns, Sunlight 2015, Newmark 2005 / 2017, Opheim 1991, FOCAL 2024, OpenSecrets 2022, plus LobbyView schema-coverage). A pre-merge factual audit on three load-bearing source summaries (Newmark 2017, FOCAL, PRI 2010) verified the r=0.04 unification rationale exactly at Newmark 2017 p.421-422. The v2 contract is the substrate every Prong-1 dispatch and every per-state release has consumed since.

2. **Prong 1 pause + gather-first pivot (2026-05-24) — then re-resumed on v2.1.** The OH 2025 pilot in May showed frontier LLMs *can* read statutes (high cross-vintage stability, low cost, no hallucinated citations), but the typed-cell schema forced lossy encodings on qualitative-trigger states (OH's "main purposes" lobbyist gate has no dollar floor for a numeric threshold cell to hold). Three successor branches were archived simultaneously (`extraction-harness-brainstorm`, `phase-c-projection-tdd`, `oh-statute-retrieval`) on the gather-first prescription: collect freeform answers first, design **v2.2** after. **That prescription was then superseded.** The Phase B Ralph loop on `wi-ralph-cpi-renewal-cadence` (archived 2026-06-05) established that per-row, **cell-type-aligned** prompt additives fix the failures the pause attributed to the schema — without schema changes. Phase A applied the pattern at scale (163 YAML additives, $0.83 verification), and Prong 1 extraction resumed on a refined **v2.1** rather than waiting on v2.2.

3. **Cross-state CPI 2015 validation — Round 1.** Branch `cross-state-cpi-2015-validation`, the empirical successor to the WI-only Ralph loop. **5 states × default-6-chunks × 2 models × 3 runs = 180 dispatches at $14.43.** Pre-fix headline: 15/30 (50%) per-(state, indicator) match against the CPI 2015 C11 published oracle. Post-Phase-1 helper vocab fix: **19/30 (63.3%)** — the +4 lift matched the failure-mode doc's Trend-1 prediction exactly, and was delivered by editing deterministic projection helpers (free) rather than re-dispatching ($14). Per-state post-fix: **NY 6/6 · WI 4/6 · CA 3/6 · TX 3/6 · OH 2/6**. σ_noise floor measured (not hypothetical): Claude 73.8%–92.9%, GPT 60.7%–88.1%. NY beat the WI training surface — the Phase A additives did not overfit. The [six failure-mode trends doc](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/blob/cross-state-cpi-2015-validation/docs/active/cross-state-cpi-2015-validation/results/20260606_failure_mode_trends.md) (2026-06-06) reframed Trends 1+6+2 as **projection-translation engineering** under the **SMR-as-canonical** principle (YAML stays statute-literal; per-rubric helpers translate; rubric-match is a CHECK, not the goal) rather than as v2.2 schema-design pressure.

4. **Prong 2 — three state chains released on `main`, three structurally different shapes.** This is the most mature prong by fellowship end.
   - **WI** (`releases/wi/`, shipped 2026-05-27): **115,229-row chain** at `releases/wi/chain/WI_chain_2025.tsv` + 6 source TSVs. Required **iterative proportional fitting** (IPF) over a bipartite (principal × lobbyist) graph with semester marginals — WI lobbyists report only aggregate hours, so the lobbyist↔bill leg has to be inferred.
   - **NY** (`releases/ny/`, shipped 2026-06-08, merged 2026-06-12): **83,786-row chain** at `releases/ny/chain/` + 5 source TSVs in-tree (~42 MB). Pure JOIN — NY discloses lobbyist→bill compensation at the per-pair grain natively. **$153,064,191 in bill-linked compensation reconciles exactly to the Phase-3 release.** 99.9% of rows resolve to an Open States bill + sponsor; 213 sponsors = full NY legislature. The `parties_lobbied` disclosed-contact edge is a separate filing-grain artifact; **98.61%** of state-legislator-titled rows resolve to `ocd-person` after the nickname matcher.
   - **OH** (`releases/oh/`, shipped 2026-06-14, merged 2026-06-15 — **the capstone**): **1,589-row chain preview** at `releases/oh/chain/` + 305-row filings TSV + 0-row gifts TSV (empirical base-rate, see below). OH's shape is a third form: per-`(filing, position, sponsor)` cross-product with **§4a position-shape normalization** (subject-only positions preserved rather than silently dropped) and **§6 OAC routing** (admin-rule citations like JCARR and OAC chapters classed separately from bills). All disclosed gift edges are extracted (the AER has a native lobbyist↔lawmaker gift/meal layer neither WI nor NY has); the slice just had none.

   **The Anna Karenina principle (2026-06-06)** captures what the three chains taught: per-state pipelines are bespoke; shared infrastructure lives downstream of extraction. WI needed IPF, NY needed neither (direct JOIN), OH reduces to JOINs over per-(agent, employer, period) AERs. Refactoring toward a state-agnostic chain composer is premature generalization and was resisted. Budget **~3 working days per state** for chain composition at TDD discipline; there is no "50 states in 80 days" extrapolation from "3 states in 5 days."

5. **Amina — backend prototype (`docs/historical/backend-prototype/`).** v0 backend shipped 2026-05-28 (FastAPI + CLI + SQLite); ingested Dan's WI release the same day (4,798 filings; DoorDash YTD $2,183,623.40 matches the release headline exactly). **2026-06-05** swapped storage SQLite → Postgres (docker compose + psycopg3) with byte-identical invariants. **2026-06-11** added the React/Vite **State Lobbying Disclosure Explorer** frontend + `GET /stats` endpoint (top spenders via JSONB cast; offset pagination; single-process static-mount). 24/24 backend tests green; live-verified against real WI data. Merged via PR #56 on 2026-06-12. **Goal #3 — the simple interface — complete and verified.**

6. **Gowrav — production architecture (`docs/active/ARCHITECTURE.md`).** The 2026-05-01 design-of-record for GraphQL + MCP production topology remains the design target downstream of the v0 prototype. Front-end work was in an awkward spot post-2026-05-24 (the typed-cell schema that earlier weekly updates flagged as Gowrav's front-end contract was archived under the v2.1 → v2.2 framing), and visible commit activity has not resumed. The architecture doc still stands as the production-tier design.

7. **Final week — OH chain composer (the capstone).** [PR #59](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/pull/59) merged 2026-06-15 as commit `ac009f5`. **Six-module pipeline** under `src/lobby_analysis/allocation/oh/` (`classify` / `load` / `chain` / `gifts` / `filings` / `cli`), shipped under TDD across four phase commits, **139/139 OH allocation tests green** (pure-logic, no DB / network / real data). Phase 0 audit confirmed OH multi-primary sponsorship is structural (40.8% of bills have ≥2 primaries, dominantly on substantive HBs / SBs); cosponsors live cleanly in `bill_sponsorships.classification == "cosponsor"` — the WI lesson does NOT apply. **The gifts-empty result is an empirical base-rate finding, not a bug:** a 2026-06-15 spot-check across all 305 cached filings found 93.8% report "No expenditures"; of the 6.2% with content, **zero** have any itemized Section II.A (gifts) or II.B (meals) rows. All disclosed expenditure activity concentrates in the non-itemized sub-$50 meal aggregate, correctly extracted to `category="entertainment"`. The brief's Rule 2 covers II.A explicitly; source data is just empty. The release READMEs were reframed in-branch with the empirical finding rather than papering over the zero. Workstream archived to `docs/historical/oh-portal-extraction/` (dir-name preserves the three-branch workstream continuity: `oh-portal-extraction` → `oh-portal-aprime-batch` → `oh-chain-composer`).

## Why this mattered

**The deliverable is the rubric-agnostic data layer, not a ranking.** Compendium 2.0 + the three state releases + the per-state coverage matrix in [`docs/STATE_COVERAGE.md`](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/blob/main/docs/STATE_COVERAGE.md) collectively represent something that didn't exist before: **a structured, per-state record of what each state's lobbying-disclosure regime actually requires + what its portal actually exposes**, indexed to a contract built from nine source rubrics on even footing. Researchers can re-aggregate this into PRI-style scorecards, FOCAL-style transparency assessments, or any other framing they prefer. We do not publish a "Corda Rubric"; we publish the substrate that makes any rubric reproducible.

**Three states is enough to falsify "one pipeline fits all," not enough to claim 50.** The Anna Karenina principle — every state's disclosure regime imposes its own data shape — was a hypothesis at WI completion and is now an operating assumption after NY (pure JOIN), OH (cross-product with OAC routing), and the abandoned uniform-chain attempt that prompted the principle in the first place. What amortizes across states is the canonical filing schema, the typed-cell schema, the projection functions, and the `releases/<state>/` format. What does NOT amortize is the per-state extraction work itself.

**The cross-state CPI validation produced honest signal at a deliberately low number.** 19/30 post-fix is not 30/30 — and under SMR-as-canonical, it should not be 30/30, because some residual is *defensible disagreement with prior-art scoring* (e.g., WI/OH IND_197: the model's literal `$0 threshold` reading of "any economic consideration" vs CPI's MODERATE — both defensible; two CPI 2015 errata candidates are documented in the failure-mode doc). The threshold for "good enough" is now expressible against measured baselines (σ_noise per state, the 19/30 match rate, the +4 free-lift demonstration) rather than asserted. That's a real upgrade from where the project started.

## What didn't ship

Honest accounting of work that was scoped but did not land before fellowship end:

- **Compendium v2.2.** The structural-gap framing that motivated the May pause was largely **mooted** by the SMR-as-canonical principle + the Phase A vocab-fix evidence: most of what v2.2 was supposed to redesign turned out to be projection-translation engineering, not schema redesign. A real v2.2 design ledger does exist — the `regime` axis gap (`docs/STATE_REGIME_SPLITTING.md`, ~50-state survey) is a genuine schema-design item that did NOT get re-decided in v2 and would belong in v2.2. But v2.2 as the gating step the May framing made it is no longer the correct framing.
- **OH full-corpus extraction run (issue [#35](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/35)).** The 316-filing slice is a preview, not a release. Full-corpus is ~$800 / ~24 hr async via Batches API + prompt caching + transient retry; the architecture is staged but the run did not dispatch in-window. Filenames end in `_preview` until it does.
- **Cross-state CPI Round 2 — CO/IL/WA/FL/NC (issue [#50](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/50)).** ~$15 dispatch; queued, not run. Trends 4 (sparse-corpus over-projection) and 5 (CPI audit-generosity) are 1-3 cells each at N=5 and need the larger sample before remediation commits.
- **WI campaign-finance leg.** FollowTheMoney scoping shipped 2026-06-03 with a sample query against LeMahieu (c-t-eid=3073941, 2,803 transactions, $609K 2022 cycle), but full ingest is gated on FTM expanded-access review (Dan emailed `info@opensecrets.org` 2026-06-03; quota wall hit; **caveat: FTM is in sunset/integration mode pending the OpenSecrets merger**). Tracked at [#43](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/43) (ingest) + [#44](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/44) (NY validation).
- **NY multi-year backfill + `lobbyist_bimonthly` fold-in.** NY chain is 2025-only; 2019→ backfill not run.
- **FOCAL Plans 3+4 (openness+timeliness + top-level projector).** Two of four FOCAL plans shipped; the remaining two are queued at [#53](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/53).
- **MI + NC disclosure-data pipelines.** MI kickoff scoped 2026-06-02 (entity + expenditure MVP, 2025 vintage) and NC access-posture re-verified 2026-06-03 (registration data free bulk; activity gated behind JS-only search — clean **legal-vs-practical** datapoint). Both branches exist (`mi-disclosure-explore`, `nc-disclosure-explore`) but are stub-status — no chain materialized.
- **Gowrav's front-end track.** No visible activity since 2026-05-01. The production-tier `ARCHITECTURE.md` design-of-record stands, but is not implemented; the v0 backend prototype is the working artifact.

## What carries forward

For whoever picks up this work — the public releases, the archived research lines, and the open task issues are all reproducible from `main`:

- **Three released chains on `main`** — `releases/wi/chain/`, `releases/ny/chain/`, `releases/oh/chain/` (preview). Each has a self-contained `README.md` documenting schema, conservation rules, provenance, and reproducer commands. None require Postgres or external services to inspect.
- **22 archived research lines under `docs/historical/`**, each with its branch's `RESEARCH_LOG.md`, `convos/`, `plans/`, and `results/` intact. The convo summaries are the institutional memory — "why was this decided" is recoverable per-decision.
- **23 open task-labeled GH issues** capture the explicit pickup queue, ranging from the high-leverage ([#35](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/35) OH full-corpus, [#50](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/50) CPI Round 2, [#43](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/43) FTM ingest) to the surgical ([#57](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/57) test-infra Postgres-fixture scoping, [#58](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/58) OH extraction-brief form-type split).
- **The backend prototype is dockerized and runnable.** `docs/historical/backend-prototype/` carries the runbook; one `docker compose up` plus an ingest call brings up the WI release as a queryable Postgres-backed API with the React explorer.
- **The Plural Policy bulk-CSV pattern is reusable cross-state.** WI, NY, and OH all use it for the lawmaker↔bill leg ([#42](https://github.com/danparshall/lobby_analysis/issues/42) tracks pulling it into a shared library).
- **Statute-line extraction is on a measured baseline.** σ_noise per state is recorded; helper-side fixes re-project stored YAML for free; the dispatch architecture is stable enough that adding the next 5 states to the validation is a one-line CLI change.
