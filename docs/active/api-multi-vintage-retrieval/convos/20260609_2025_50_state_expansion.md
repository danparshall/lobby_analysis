### CF finding (load-bearing)

The empirical pattern across this session:
- AL pass-1 probe (1 fetch): clean.
- Canary 1 (3 concurrent × 3-pass each, ~10-15 fetches): clean.
- Batch 1 (3 concurrent × 3-pass each, ~25-30 cumulative fetches before dispatch): 2/3 CF-blocked.

This is **not really about instantaneous concurrency** — canary 1 was also 3-concurrent. It looks more like **cumulative session fetch count crossing a CF threshold**. AZ specifically had pass-1 work then CF fired on pass-2 a few seconds later — consistent with a per-IP rate-limit budget that decays over time, not a hard ban.

Mitigation menu (per AskUserQuestion #4) — user chose **stop session, resume later with fresh CF state**. Other options surfaced: 2-concurrent + 15-min cooldowns; 1-concurrent + 5-min cooldowns; wire stealth-playwright into `subagent_fetch_save.py` before next attempt.

## Provisional findings

- **Method B handles structurally diverse regimes well at opus tier.** Validated on: single-body single-leaf (AL), two-body two-title (LA), unicameral flat-chapter (NE), deep-title-but-actually-shallow (VA). Regime priors in dispatch prompts can be approximate — the canary reads the actual Justia TSV and self-corrects to ground truth (LA section ranges, VA chapter renumbering, NE section-range truncation).
- **Per-canary cost is much higher than the $0.10 estimate I anchored the budget on.** Avg 92K subagent tokens / canary = ~$0.50–$1.00 at opus rates. The 5-state 6-05 gap-fill session was apparently cheaper than this; possible drivers: longer regime priors in this session's dispatches, deeper TSV traversal at 2025 vintage, opus-4-7 vs opus-4-6.
- **CF rate-limit appears cumulative, not just concurrency-driven.** Future fan-outs should plan for either (a) network-surface diversity (VPN, hotspot, different times of day), (b) stealth-playwright as a more durable fetch path, or (c) batched-with-cooldown pacing that lets the rate-limit budget decay.
- **VA regime prior is stale in the dispatch corpus.** "Chapter 4.4" was correct at some prior vintage but the 2025 codification moved lobbying to "Chapter 4 Article 3." Worth fixing in any persisted regime-prior reference.
